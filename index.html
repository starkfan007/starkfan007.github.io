2026 Hybrid VST for Extremely Dark Image Denoising
A robust, edge-friendly variance-stabilizing transform that uses a linear mapping in the ultra-low-light region and smoothly transitions to a VST mapping at higher signal levels. The design prioritizes unbiased color and luminance reconstruction under black-level drift and sensor calibration errors.
2026 FPN Correction for Temporally Correlated Sensor Noise
An analysis and calibration strategy for fixed-pattern noise in 3D denoising. The approach aligns SNR guidance with the actual multi-frame noise statistics, improving temporal denoising behavior and reducing artifacts caused by a mismatch between ideal and real sensor noise.
2025 YOND: Diffusion-Inspired Real-Raw Image Denoising
Research on interpreting neural denoisers from a diffusion perspective: using noise-level conditioning, iterative denoising, and interpretable VST-based noise estimation to improve generalization across raw sensors while retaining controllable detail.
